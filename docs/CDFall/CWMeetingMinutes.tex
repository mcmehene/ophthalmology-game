\section{Cognitive Walkthrough Questions}
These are the questions that will be asked during the cognitive walkthrough. It is possible, 
depending on the evaluators that questions get skipped. But responses at times can provide
implied answers with additional feedback.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Will users try to achieve the right result?} 
    In other words, do users understand that the action (step) at hand is needed to reach their larger goal?
    \item \textbf{Will users notice that the correct action is available?} 
    In other words, is the interactive element that achieves the step visible or easily findable?
    \item \textbf{Will users associate the correct action with the result they’re trying to achieve?} 
    Perhaps the right button is visible, but will users understand the label and will they know to engage with it?
    \item \textbf{After the action is performed, will users see that progress is made toward the goal?} 
    Based on what occurs after the action is taken, will users know that this action was correct and 
    helped them make progress toward their larger goal?
\end{enumerate}
